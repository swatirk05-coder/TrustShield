# TrustShield
TrustShield General Insurance is a unified digital insurance platform designed to simplify the entire insurance journey. Users can discover, compare, purchase, and manage various insurance policies, including health, motor, travel, and home insurance. The platform also enables policy renewals, claim tracking, premium calculations, AI-powered policy recommendations, and secure customer support. With an intuitive dashboard and seamless digital experience, it helps customers make informed decisions while providing quick access to policy information and insurance services, making insurance more accessible, transparent, and user-friendly
